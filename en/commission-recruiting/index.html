FAQ

If a retained search does not result in a hire, do we have to pay?+
Permanent recruitment and executive search are mostly on a success-fee basis, so in principle there is no charge unless a candidate is placed and starts work. Actual fees follow the terms agreed in the engagement contract.
Is there a guarantee period or replacement guarantee?+
We agree on a guarantee period before the engagement. If the hired candidate leaves within that period, Step1ne provides a free replacement; specific terms follow what both parties agree in the engagement contract.
How are staffing fees calculated?+
Staffing is quoted based on headcount, job duties and scope of service. Fees are explained clearly during discussions and written into the contract — a transparent process with no hidden costs. Contact us on LINE for an initial quote.
When is commissioned recruitment the right fit?+
Retained recruitment is a good fit when a role must be kept confidential (replacing a sitting executive, or key hires ahead of a reorganization), when the people you want are currently employed and won't apply on their own, or when your in-house HR team is stretched and needs to outsource.
