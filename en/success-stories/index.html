Success Stories: Real Results from Companies That Recruited Through Step1ne

We don't just fill a one-off hiring need; we help build a company's long-term talent strategy. Below are real cases where Step1ne helped companies complete commissioned recruitment (anonymized by field).

Case 1: A fab construction project urgently needed BIM staff. By changing "must have experience" to "willing to learn from scratch," it took 17 days from application to start

Construction & Fab Projects

Why companies keep failing to find people

A major tech manufacturer's fab construction project needed a batch of BIM modelers — and needed them on board within one month. But experienced people who know Revit / AutoCAD and are willing to be stationed long-term at fab sites in central Taiwan are scarce to begin with, and everyone is competing for them.

Where the requirements got stuck

The original mix of conditions put people off: it was planned as a staffing role, and an early version mentioned housing, which was later changed to a rent subsidy only. When job seekers saw "staffing," "out of town," and "no housing," most simply skipped it.

How consultants clarify things again

The consultant renegotiated with the employer point by point: switching to permanent employment, focusing screening on work attitude and career stability, and stating clearly “no experience required, a dedicated mentor until you're up to speed, and no binding contract for training.” With job seekers, we laid out the real picture first — about 80% drafting in the office and 20% on site, no dormitory but a rent subsidy, and overtime required during the project — so that those who came had thought it through. The person hired this time was a career changer from an unrelated field who had never used AutoCAD; what he cared about was whether the job had a future. Only after the consultant explained that once the plant construction project ended he would move on to other projects, and that his skills could build up over the long term, did he decide to move forward.

Result

17 days from application to start: AI initial interview and consultant call on the day you apply, first interview and offer on day 6, start on day 17.

Case 2: Assistant to an executive in Japan — clarifying "Taiwan and Japan" as "based in Japan, housing at your own expense" is what found the right candidates

Overseas Executive Support

Why companies keep failing to find people

A company was looking for an executive assistant who could interpret Japanese on the spot and support an executive with matters large and small, based in Japan. People with business-meeting-level Japanese, executive assistant experience and a willingness to relocate to Japan are rare to begin with.

Where the requirements got stuck

The job was first described as "Taiwan and Japan," so candidates assumed it meant traveling back and forth. It also never made clear whether only Japanese nationals would be considered. As a result, people came in with the wrong expectations: some thought they would not need to relocate, and others thought Taiwanese candidates had no chance.

How consultants clarify things again

Our consultant went back to the company to confirm two things: first, this is a permanent posting in Japan, not a back-and-forth arrangement; second, Taiwanese candidates are welcome—the real hurdle isn't nationality but whether you're willing to live in Japan at your own expense. The job title and interview questions were revised accordingly: we directly confirm your Japanese level, residence status, whether you can accept self-funded housing in Tokyo and a one-year renewable contract, and whether you've worked out your own cost of living in Japan.

Result

41 days from application to start: AI first interview completed on the day of application, recommended after a consultant's phone check on day 10, started on day 41.
