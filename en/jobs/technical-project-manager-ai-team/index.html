Job description

Project scope and timeline

  • Define the scope of AI and data-driven projects, create complete project plans and timelines, and track progress and adjust as needed

Turn AI models into product features

  • Work with machine learning engineers, data analysts and software engineers to turn AI models into real features and applications

Risk and transparency

  • Track AI project outcomes and keep model development, testing, and deployment transparent; identify and reduce the risks and dependencies specific to AI projects

The bridge between technical and non-technical teams

  • Act as the main bridge between the technical team and non-technical stakeholders, sharing regular updates and managing expectations

FAQ

What is the interview process?+

Three rounds, all of which can be done online: a quick call with HR (about 1 hour), a competency interview with the department head (about 1.5 hours), and a values interview with HR (about 1 hour).

Why does AI keep coming up?+

The hiring unit places great weight on experience "actually bringing generative AI / coding agents into your workflow," and it will come up in the interview. We recommend preparing one real example you can talk through in detail.

Which company is it?+

The hiring company prefers not to disclose its name at the initial contact stage. Once you pass the initial assessment, your consultant will explain the company's background, products, and team in full when they contact you.
