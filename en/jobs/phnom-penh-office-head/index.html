Phnom Penh Office HeadHead of a multinational medical supplies group's Cambodia operation: manage the Phnom Penh office and connect headquarters with the local team

A multinational medical supplies manufacturing group with plants in Cambodia and the Philippines and its group management headquarters in Taipei. This role is based in Phnom Penh, running the local office's daily operations and managing the team. You will be the key link between headquarters and the Cambodia team: tracking progress, handling surprises, looking after local partners, and making sure headquarters' requirements are actually carried out on the ground. The level ranges from manager to vice president, set according to your experience.

Job description

Office operations and people management

  • Oversee day-to-day operations and administration of the Phnom Penh office
  • Assign work, track performance, and develop local talent

The coordination point between headquarters and the local team

  • Coordinate between headquarters, the local team, and partners
  • Track sales and supply chain matters and help resolve cross-department issues

Purchasing, logistics, and import/export tracking

  • Coordinate purchasing, logistics, and import/export operations
  • Track orders, documents, and delivery progress, and report issues immediately

Local relationships and compliance coordination

  • Maintain relationships with local suppliers and external organizations
  • Work with professionals on legal, HR, finance and tax, and compliance matters

Requirements

Required
  • Bachelor's degree or above (business administration or international trade preferred)
  • 15+ years of experience in business management or international trade, including 5+ years on overseas assignment
  • A track record of cross-departmental integration and broader operations management within a group
  • Hands-on experience using AI, and able to handle daily operations and unexpected issues independently overseas
  • Strong Chinese and English communication skills; able to lead work meetings, write reports, and coordinate with external partners
  • Able to work in Cambodia long-term
Preferred qualifications
  • Work experience in Cambodia or Southeast Asia and familiarity with the local business environment
  • Speaks Khmer
  • Experience in manufacturing such as medical consumables, protective equipment, or textiles and apparel, or hands-on import/export experience

Why this opportunity

Site head, from manager to vice president level depending on experience

Reports directly to group headquarters

Serve as the main point of contact between HQ and the Cambodia team, working hands-on in supply chain and international trade operations.
