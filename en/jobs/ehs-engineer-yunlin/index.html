Occupational Safety and Health EngineerHigh-tech plant construction · Based in Huwei, Yunlin · Fully covered housing

Manage occupational safety and health at construction sites for new and expanded high-tech plant laboratories — stationed in Huwei, Yunlin, with fully paid housing, day shifts only (no rotation), and work that spans all of Taiwan.

Why this role is worth a look: fully company-provided housing with no rent or utilities to pay, subsidies for fuel and trips home, occupational safety and health certification training fully paid by the company, and day shifts from 08:30–17:30 with no rotation, plus a range of bonuses including year-end, mid-year, Dragon Boat Festival, and Mid-Autumn Festival bonuses.

Job description

On-site safety and health management

  • Carry out the Occupational Safety and Health Management Plan
  • Run daily toolbox meetings
  • Site safety and health inspection rounds and contractor management
  • Site surveys

Documents and administration

  • Handle contractor work orders and site entry permit applications
  • Prepare occupational safety and health forms
  • Complete tasks assigned by your supervisor according to project progress

Requirements

Required
  • 3+ years of occupational safety and health experience
  • Associate degree or above, any major
  • Basic document skills
  • Communication and coordination with internal teams and contractors, and teamwork
  • Able to be assigned to the company's current project locations (currently Huwei, Yunlin; assignments cover Taoyuan/Hsinchu/Miaoli, Taichung and Yunlin)
  • A standard heavy motorcycle license for daily commuting
Preferred qualifications
  • Class A Occupational Safety and Health Supervisor certificate (construction industry, or general industry)
  • Occupational safety and health certifications, such as the Class B Occupational Safety and Health Management technician certificate
  • Senior on-site management experience is a plus

Why this opportunity

Fully provided housing — no need to find your own place

When assigned to out-of-area projects, the company fully covers dormitory housing—no rent or utilities out of your pocket.

Commuting costs subsidized

Commuting from nearby cities and counties comes with a fuel allowance, and accepting an assignment comes with a travel-home allowance, so you won't have to absorb commuting costs yourself.

Certification costs paid by the company

Occupational safety and health certification and training are fully subsidized, so earning the Class A Safety Supervisor or Level B Technician certificate costs you nothing.

High-tech plant construction project experience

The work is on new builds and expansions of laboratories in high-tech facilities, and this on-site experience is highly valued in both construction and facility engineering.

Bonuses beyond year-end

There are bonuses at year-end, mid-year, Dragon Boat Festival and Mid-Autumn Festival — extra income several times a year.

FAQ

Where can I be assigned?+

Currently stationed in Huwei, Yunlin. The company's project sites are in the Taoyuan–Hsinchu–Miaoli, Taichung, and Yunlin areas, with assignments based on project schedules; dormitory housing is provided when you are reassigned.

Can I apply without an occupational safety and health certificate?+

Certifications are a plus, not a requirement; the main bar is 3+ years of hands-on occupational safety and health experience. The company fully subsidizes certification training, so you can take the exam after joining.

Why is a scooter/motorcycle license required?+

Needed for getting between the plant and housing and moving around the work site, and handy for daily commuting. Housing is usually not far from the plant.

Is there a lot of overtime?+

Day shift 08:30–17:30 with 2 days off per week, and as a rule no rotating shifts. Overtime is always compensated with overtime pay or compensatory leave as required by law; the actual situation depends on project progress and will be explained at the interview.
