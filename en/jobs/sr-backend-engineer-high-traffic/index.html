Job description

Technical decision-maker

  • Model complex business logic as systems and propose the best architecture

The last line of defense for quality

  • Review AI-generated architecture and solve deep performance issues such as technical debt and deadlocks under high concurrency

Automation strategy guidance

  • Guide business units on safely introducing Agentic Workflows into high-load systems, so system robustness scales with development speed

FAQ

Is it okay if I don't know C#?+

Yes, but you need to switch languages and get up to speed within your first month; this is a probation review criterion.

What is the interview process?+

A pre-assessment plus four rounds: online Leetcode (about 1.5 hours), on-site system architecture whiteboard plus AI usage (about 1.5 hours), online HR values interview (about 1 hour), and on-site AI agent whiteboard (about 1 hour).

Why does AI keep coming up?+

The hiring unit places great weight on experience "actually bringing generative AI / coding agents into your workflow," and it will come up in the interview. We recommend preparing one real example you can talk through in detail.

Which company is it?+

The hiring company prefers not to disclose its name at the initial contact stage. Once you pass the initial assessment, your consultant will explain the company's background, products, and team in full when they contact you.
