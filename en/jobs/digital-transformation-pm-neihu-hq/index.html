Job description

Needs diagnosis and process mapping

  • Go deep into the front lines of the group's business units and uncover operational bottlenecks through interviews and process analysis
  • Turn vague business needs into clearly structured, technically feasible blueprints for digital and AI implementation

Cross-department coordination and consensus building

  • Work with business unit heads, frontline users and R&D teams, balancing different views to build consensus for transformation
  • Explain the value of solutions to non-technical managers while communicating on engineering details with the R&D team

Plan specifications and implementation

  • Define milestones and acceptance criteria for each transformation phase
  • Break down business pain points into specifications the engineering team can easily understand and build (PRD / User Stories)

R&D collaboration and project control

  • Work with engineers and architects to assess technical implementation difficulty, data integration hurdles and the feasibility of rolling out AI tools
  • Drive projects end to end, proactively remove friction and delay risks in cross-team collaboration, and ensure on-time launches

Requirements

Required
  • 2+ years of project management experience in enterprise system implementation, digital transformation, process automation (RPA / Workflow Automation), or AI adoption, with real cases of guiding business units to go live
  • Mature cross-functional communication and conflict-resolution skills: able to explain value to non-technical executives, address concerns from the sales side, and hold engineering discussions with R&D teams
  • Able to independently map workflows, write specifications (PRD / User Stories), and run system acceptance testing
  • Understand the capabilities and limits of current generative AI and workflow automation tools, with the judgment to assess how feasible they are to adopt in real-world scenarios
