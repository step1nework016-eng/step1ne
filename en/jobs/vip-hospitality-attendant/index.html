Job description

Permanent employment. The service model follows the VIP butler and concierge standard of a five-star hotel (Butler/Concierge): you welcome distinguished guests and provide a refined, attentive, home-away-from-home high-end service experience. Because guests value their privacy, their identities and conversations must be kept confidential.

Guest reception and service

  • Host VIP guests and deliver attentive, warm, high-end service that makes them feel at home

Relationship management

  • Maintain VIP relationships and build long-term trust and rapport

Handling situations on site

  • Quickly grasp and properly handle on-site incidents and guest needs

Environment and supplies management

  • Keep the premises clean and refined, and make sure facilities and equipment work properly
  • Manage spare parts and resource inventory systematically and help the team complete daily operations

Requirements

Required
  • Good communication and emotional management skills
  • Strong sense of responsibility and empathy
  • Basic Office skills (Word, Excel)
  • Able to provide a police criminal record certificate (certificate of good conduct)
  • Chinese communication is sufficient
Preferred qualifications
  • Experience in high-end hospitality such as guesthouses or hotels is a plus
  • Detail-oriented, emotionally steady, resilient under pressure, and respectful of privacy and confidentiality is a plus

Why this opportunity

Attractive starting pay

Starting from NT$35,000 without experience and NT$40,000 with experience, set according to experience and interview results; seasoned or highly qualified candidates can negotiate up to NT$50,000. Those with service industry experience start higher — your effort gets noticed.

Regular day shifts, no late nights

Working hours are a fixed 10:00–19:00 day shift — no flipping day and night, and a steady pace of life.

Expanding — every opening is an opportunity

The team is hiring 2–3 new members as the business expands, making this a great time to join on the growth curve.

Fast interviews, fast start

Start with an initial chat with AI, and once a consultant confirms the terms, an interview with the company can be arranged right away. The process is clear and quick — you could start work soon after the interview.

No specific education or experience required; ability matters more than background

Education and past experience don't matter: if you have strong communication, emotional composure, and a passion for service, you have a real chance to join.

FAQ

Can I apply without relevant experience?+

Yes — education and work history don't matter. We care more about communication, emotional management and passion for service; those with hospitality experience start at a higher salary.

Starting from NT$35,000 without experience and NT$40,000 with experience, set according to experience and interview results; seasoned or highly qualified candidates can reach up to NT$50,000. Final pay is as confirmed by both sides after the interview.

Yes. Because the work involves guests' privacy, in line with hotel and private venue industry practice, guests' identities and conversations must be kept confidential.
