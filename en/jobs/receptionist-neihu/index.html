FAQ

Who employs me in this job, and how is the contract signed?+

During the assignment, you sign a formal employment contract with Step1ne, while the work takes place at the host company's site, with tasks assigned by your on-site supervisor.

Are a staffing employee's salary, labor insurance, and health insurance the same as regular employees'?+

Yes, it is the same. Salary, labor and health insurance, annual leave, and other benefits all follow the Labor Standards Act, just like any other employee.

How are shifts scheduled? Will I need to rotate onto overnight shifts?+

There are two shifts (09:00–18:00 and 09:30–18:30), rotated within the team, with two days off per week and no overnight or weekend shifts.

Is Step1ne a licensed recruitment agency?+

Step1ne is a brand of 德仁管理顧問有限公司 and holds an employment services license issued by the Taipei City Government (北市就服字第0363號). Registered address: 3F, No. 54-7, Sec. 3, Kangning Rd., Neihu Dist., Taipei City.

What does the interview process look like?+

A Step1ne consultant first holds an initial conversation and re-checks your qualifications; once you are confirmed as a fit, we arrange an interview with the company's HR team. The final hiring decision rests with the company.
