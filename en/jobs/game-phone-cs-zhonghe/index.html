FAQ

Is this a staffing role or a permanent position?+

This is a six-month staffing position. After you start, you sign a formal employment contract with Step1ne (a brand of 德仁管理顧問有限公司), and salary, labor/health insurance and annual leave are all handled under the Labor Standards Act.

Can I work remotely?+

This role requires working on-site in Zhonghe, New Taipei; remote work is not available. Monthly salary NT$31,000, plus a quarterly bonus and on-duty allowance.

Will the six-month contract be renewed when it ends?+

Renewal depends on the project and on both parties' wishes, so we can't guarantee renewal or conversion to a permanent role.

Can I apply without game-industry customer support experience?+

The hiring team specifically requires phone or chat support experience in the gaming industry, so it will be harder to pass with no relevant experience at all. If you are interested but your experience does not match, you can ask the consultant to match you with other customer support jobs that do not require industry experience.

How does the interview process work?+

A-Cai, our AI interviewer, first gets to know you, then a consultant holds a confidential first conversation. If it's a good fit, we arrange an interview with the company and settle the terms; the final hiring decision is made by the company.
