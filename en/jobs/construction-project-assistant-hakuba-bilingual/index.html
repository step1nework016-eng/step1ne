Construction Project Assistant (Hakuba, Japan; Chinese–Japanese)Support the project manager on a construction project in Hakuba: track progress, coordinate contractors and the site, and interpret and translate between Chinese and Japanese. Full-time, two full days off every week

This role mainly supports the project manager in driving a construction project in Hakuba, Japan: tracking construction progress, communicating with the site team and vendors, and handling Chinese–Japanese translation. We are looking for someone who can actively move the project forward, follow up on progress proactively, coordinate local resources, and report to their manager promptly. A background in architecture, construction, or engineering projects is required, along with business-level Chinese and Japanese. An architect's license is not required, and any field of study is welcome.

Job description

  • Help the project manager track architectural planning, design, and construction progress
  • Liaise and coordinate with Japanese architects, construction companies, contractors, and relevant local parties
  • Attend discussions and meetings with architects and provide interpreting
  • Translate construction-related documents, drawings, and contracts
  • Coordinate furniture and interior design vendors for company-owned assets (such as villas)
  • Apply your understanding of Japanese construction practice to fill gaps in the team's expertise and offer advice
  • Serve as the point of contact between the Taiwan managers/team and the local team in Japan

FAQ

Is the first interview conducted by AI?+

Yes. Step1ne's initial interview is conducted by A-Cai, our AI interviewer, through online text chat. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes, and you choose the time, so there's no need to take time off. Afterward, a human consultant reviews the conversation before contacting you. Your information will not be sent to the hiring company without your consent.

What is the salary?+

Negotiable based on experience; your consultant will explain the details during the initial phone conversation. There is a salary review system, and bonuses depend on the company's business performance.

Do I need an architect license or a degree in architecture?+

No, any field of study is welcome. You do need a background or foundational experience in architecture, construction, or engineering projects; a degree in architecture or a related field is a plus.

What level of Japanese do I need?+

Business-level communication skills are required: you should be able to hold meetings with local vendors and handle interpreting and document translation.

Will I be based in Hakuba full-time? How does housing work?+

This role supports a construction project in Hakuba and is based in Hakuba full time. The company provides support for relocating to Hakuba from within Japan. Your consultant will explain the housing and support details during the initial conversation.

I'm based in Taiwan. Can I apply?+

You are welcome to apply first. Your consultant will check work visa and residence status arrangements with the hiring company and get back to you.
