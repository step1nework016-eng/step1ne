Job description

Social media management and content planning

  • Run Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, the official LINE account, Google My Business and other platforms
  • Plan digital and content direction based on the company's annual and sales targets

Website Content, Image and Video Editor

  • Update images, copy, and video content on the official website
  • Communicate and collaborate with web designers

Short-form video production

  • Independently write short video scripts and set up shots
  • Handle shooting, editing, music, effects, subtitles, and other post-production

Requirements

Required
  • 3+ years of experience in social media marketing or video production
  • Associate degree or above, any major
  • Basic graphic design skills
  • Short-form video editing skills

Preferred qualifications
  • Large heavy motorcycle license required

Why this opportunity

Create content for a century-old American heavy motorcycle brand

FAQ

Pay for this role is negotiable, with regular monthly pay of NT$40,000 or more; the actual figure will be confirmed after the interview based on your experience and skills.

Yes — this job does not require riding a heavy motorcycle. If you do hold a large heavy motorcycle license, it is a plus.

This role requires you to independently plan, shoot, and edit short videos. You can confirm the actual team setup with your consultant at the interview.
