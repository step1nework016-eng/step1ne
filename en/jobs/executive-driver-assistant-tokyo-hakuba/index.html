Executive Assistant to the President & Chauffeur (Tokyo / Hakuba)JPY 4,000,000–7,000,000/year · based in Kudan, Tokyo · about 60% of winter in Hakuba, Nagano

A Japanese real estate development company (the Japanese entity of a Taiwanese real estate development group) is looking for an Executive Assistant to the President who will also serve as the President's driver. The heart of this role is executive support: helping the President and the management team with schedules, Taiwan–Japan communication and ad hoc tasks. Driving the President is part of the job, but not the only focus. On weekdays you will work at the office in Kudankita, Chiyoda, Tokyo, and during the winter peak season about 60% of your working time is expected to be spent on business trips to, or stationed at, the base in Hakuba, Nagano. Business-level Japanese and Chinese are required, and you must be able to drive.

Job description

Day-to-day support for the president and executives

  • Assist the President and management team with day-to-day administrative matters and ad hoc tasks
  • Staying on top of the progress and details of key schedules, meetings, and external events
  • Coordinate with other departments and external parties as your manager requires

Scheduling and business travel

  • Manage the schedules of the President and senior management and coordinate timing with all parties
  • Arrange business travel transport, accommodation and itineraries
  • Preparing meeting times, venues, and materials

Two-way Taiwan–Japan Communication

  • Handle day-to-day information flow between the Japanese entity and the Taiwan head office
  • Support meeting communication and visitor reception in both Chinese and Japanese
  • Coordinate schedules and matters with clients, partners, and external parties

Accompanying and driving

  • Drive the president, executives and key guests in the company car
  • Travel between the Tokyo office, client visits, and the Tokyo/Hakuba sites
  • Planning routes, checking road conditions, and arranging parking in advance

Hakuba site support (winter)

  • In winter, support Hakuba operations and the executives' schedule
  • Support on-site liaison, administrative tasks and ad hoc requests
  • Adapt to a highly mobile work schedule

FAQ

Do I really need to stay in Hakuba for long stretches in winter?+

Yes. Your contractual workplace is the office in Kudankita, Chiyoda, Tokyo, but during the winter peak season about 60% of your working time is expected to be spent on business trips to, or stationed at, the base in Hakuba, Nagano. This is the single most important thing to confirm you can accommodate before applying.

Is this mainly a driver role or an executive assistant role?+

It is mainly an executive assistant role that also includes driving. The core work is executive support—scheduling, coordinating communication between Taiwan and Japan, and ad hoc requests. Driving the president, executives and guests in the company car is part of the job, but not the only focus.

Can I apply without professional driving experience?+

Yes. We look at the overall combination of executive support skills, Chinese–Japanese communication, driving ability, and flexibility, rather than judging only by years as a professional driver. If you have an executive assistant or administrative support background, hold a standard (futsu) driver's license, and have real on-road driving experience, you're welcome to apply even if you've never worked as a full-time driver.

With an annual salary of JPY 4,000,000–7,000,000, how much will I actually get?+

The page shows the total annual salary range, which is set based on experience. Your consultant will explain how the salary is structured in full during the first phone conversation.

Do I have to drive myself?+

Yes. The job includes driving the president, executives and guests in the company car, so you need a standard driver's license and real on-road driving experience, and must be able to drive safely on your own. In winter, Hakuba means snowy and mountain roads.

How good does my Japanese need to be? Will I use Chinese too?+

You'll need Japanese good enough to communicate in real time in meetings and when receiving visitors, and to handle business correspondence. Chinese is also a working language, because this role handles communication between the Japanese subsidiary and the Taiwan head office.

What is the application process?+

After you apply, you will first have an initial interview with A-Cai, our AI interview assistant (this stage is conducted by AI), and then a consultant will confirm your requirements and interest. The hiring company's process consists of a document review and HR interview, an interview with members of the Japanese entity, and finally an interview with the person in charge (conducted in Chinese).

Will the working hours be very irregular?+

The standard schedule is 10:00–19:00 with 8 actual working hours a day, two full days off every week, and an average of 21 working days a month. Start and end times shift, however, to match senior management's schedule.
