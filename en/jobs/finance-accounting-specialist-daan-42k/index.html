Finance & Accounting Assistant / Finance Administration Specialist (Taipei)Support payments and receipts, account reconciliation, and Excel financial reports across multiple companies' accounts and cross-department coordination

This role supports the company's day-to-day finance, accounting, and administrative work, including accounts management for multiple companies and cross-department financial coordination. We are looking for someone careful, steady, proactive, and able to execute, who can assist their manager with daily finance work and reliably follow up on each task.

Job description

Day-to-day finance and accounting tasks

  • Assist with daily receipts and payments, payment requests, reimbursements, and expense reconciliation
  • Organize bank transaction details, remittance records, and related financial documents
  • Assist with organizing, categorizing, and filing accounting records
  • Assist with reconciling and tracking accounts receivable and payable
  • Provide relevant accounting data to the accountants and finance manager as needed

Financial statements and data management

  • Assist in preparing and updating Excel financial management reports
  • Regularly compile income, expense, and cost details for each company
  • Assist with organizing bank account balances, cash flows, and payment records
  • Maintain complete financial records, ensuring data is accurate and traceable

Cross-company finance administration and coordination

  • Assist in compiling financial data for companies in Taiwan, Japan, and overseas
  • Track the progress of billing, payments, and accounting documents
  • Assist with communication with internal colleagues, external accountants, and relevant partner contacts
  • Assist your manager with other finance administration and project support tasks as needed

Requirements

Required
  • 1–3+ years of experience in finance, accounting, or related administrative work
  • Familiar with basic Excel operations and common functions
  • Basic accounting knowledge, with an understanding of payment, expense, and reconciliation processes
  • Careful and responsible, with a strong focus on numerical accuracy
  • Good communication and coordination skills
  • Proactively tracks work progress and promptly reports issues to managers
  • Able to handle multiple tasks at once, with good time management and prioritization skills
Preferred qualifications
  • Previous experience at an accounting firm or in a corporate finance department
  • Experience handling bookkeeping for multiple companies
  • Familiar with bank remittances, foreign currency transactions, or cross-border payments
  • Basic English reading and email writing skills
  • Experience with ERP, accounting systems, or organizing financial statements

Why this opportunity

Clear division of roles, so you won't carry financial decisions alone

This role assists with daily finance work and tracks task progress; you will not be expected to independently take full responsibility for financial decisions or tax filings.

Exposure to the accounting practices of multiple companies

You will compile financial data for companies in Taiwan, Japan, and offshore, and also work with external accountants and partner contacts.

FAQ

Will I be solely responsible for financial decisions or tax filing?+

No. This role assists the manager with daily finance work and does not require independently handling full financial decisions or tax filing.

What is the salary?+

Where is the actual workplace?+

The exact office location will be confirmed at the interview.

What is the application process?+

After you apply, Step1ne's AI interviewer will conduct an initial interview (you will be told beforehand that this part is run by AI). A consultant will then review the summary and screen your resume, and if you pass, an interview will be arranged.

I'm currently employed. Will my current company find out that I applied?+

No. Step1ne will never send your information without your consent, and will not contact your current employer. Interviews can be scheduled after your working hours.
