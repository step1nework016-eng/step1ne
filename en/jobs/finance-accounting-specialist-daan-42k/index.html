Finance & Accounting Specialist (Monthly NT$42–50K, Da'an, Taipei)A new finance and accounting role on the Taiwan side of a Japanese real estate development group: day-to-day bookkeeping, voucher reconciliation, invoices and payments, and monthly reports, with guidance and review from the current finance manager

This is a newly created finance and accounting execution role on the Taiwan side of a Japanese real estate development group. The team already has a finance manager who oversees the books; this role is here to share the day-to-day workload: organizing income and expense data, reconciling vouchers, handling the administrative flow for invoices and payments, and preparing monthly accounts and basic management reports. Professional judgment and review are handled by the manager, so you will not have to carry financial management, multi-country taxation, or investment evaluation on your own. What we look at is the accounting work you have actually handled, not your number of years; fluent Japanese, experience with Japanese projects, or a Big Four background are not requirements. The group has future real estate development projects in Japan, so you may gradually get involved in accounting across entities and with overseas contacts, but these are not skills you need on day one.

Job description

Daily bookkeeping and document processing

  • Organize daily income and expense data and check supporting documents to confirm receipts match the books
  • Support the admin process for payment requests and payments, and track payment status

Monthly bookkeeping and report preparation

  • Help prepare monthly books, financial details and basic management reports
  • Check data completeness and, when you find discrepancies, compile them into an issue list for your supervisor

Cross-department liaison and process improvement

  • Work with the finance lead and other departments to follow up on missing receipts and documents
  • Help create and update accounting forms, work records and internal processes

Compile project expenses and costs

  • Under your supervisor's guidance, organize expenses, costs and payment status for different businesses or projects
  • Support other finance and admin tasks and cross-department coordination

You may gradually get involved in the future (not a requirement at hiring)

  • Compile accounting records for Japanese real estate projects
  • Work with overseas accounting / tax contacts
  • Track intercompany transactions, project budgets, and funding progress
  • This is introduced step by step based on business progress and your situation, so you do not need it when you join

Requirements

Required
  • Basic hands-on finance or accounting experience (backgrounds as an accounting specialist, finance assistant or accounting assistant handling general bookkeeping, receivables/payables or expense reconciliation are a good fit)
  • Familiar with general bookkeeping and data reconciliation, with basic Excel and word-processing skills
  • Detail-oriented and accuracy-focused, proactively raising issues and following them through to closure
  • Eager to learn and adaptable, able to communicate and coordinate across departments
  • Comfortable with some processes still being built, and willing to discuss and gradually improve them with the existing team
Preferred qualifications
  • A degree in finance or accounting, or experience using ERP / accounting systems
  • Have helped with month-end closing, receivables and payables, expense management, reports or cost analysis
  • Related experience in real estate, construction, hotels and hospitality, or project-based businesses
  • Able to read financial materials in English
  • Exposure to Japan-related or multi-entity accounting

Why this opportunity

A new position, but you won't be left to figure it out on your own

This is a new hands-on role created as the Taiwan side of the business grows. The team already has a finance manager overseeing the books, who is responsible for professional judgment and review. You will share the day-to-day workload — not carry financial management, multi-country tax matters or investment decisions on your own.

We look at what you have done, not the number of years

The hiring unit has made clear that a fixed number of years of experience will not be used to rule anyone out, and it does not require fluent Japanese, Japan project experience, a Big Four background, overseas tax law expertise, or independent ownership of IRR / cash flow analysis. In the interview you will be asked what accounting work you have actually handled and how you check payment and expense data for errors.

Processes are still growing, so your input will be heard

Some accounting forms and workflows are still being built. That means you won't just follow an existing SOP — you can propose more practical ways of doing things, discuss them and build them into the process.

Future opportunities to broaden your scope to international projects

The group has real estate development projects in Japan, so over time you may gradually work on inter-company transactions, coordination with overseas accounting and tax contacts, and project budget and cash tracking. None of this experience is needed when you join; these are skills you can grow into.

Employed and insured in Taiwan

You are employed in Taiwan and paid a Taiwan salary, with labor and health insurance under Taiwan regulations. The workplace is in Da'an District, Taipei, and no posting to Japan is required.

FAQ

I don't speak Japanese and haven't worked on Japan-related projects. Can I still apply?+

Yes. The hiring team has explicitly excluded fluent Japanese and Japan project experience from the required criteria, and daily work is mainly in Chinese. Being able to read English financial materials is a plus, not a requirement.

My experience isn't long—will I be screened out on years?+

The hiring unit says it does not screen people out by a fixed number of years. What matters is which accounting tasks you have actually handled and how you reconcile data. If you have done general bookkeeping, accounts receivable and payable, and expense reconciliation, you are a good fit to apply; your consultant will check with you in the first conversation whether your hands-on depth matches this role.

The monthly salary is NT$42,000–50,000. How much will I actually take home?+

Your pay will be set within this range based on your experience. The consultant will explain how the salary is structured and the actual year-end and festival bonuses during the first phone call.

Does this role require independently handling financial management or investment decisions?+

No. The current finance manager provides guidance and review. CFO-level strategy and investment decisions, expertise in overseas tax law, and independent IRR / cash flow analysis are not part of this role's requirements.

Will I be transferred to Japan later on?+

No. This is a Taiwan-based position: your employment, salary, and insurance are all in Taiwan, and you'll work in Da'an District, Taipei. Japanese real estate projects are a possible area of future collaboration, not an overseas assignment.

What is the application process?+

After you apply, Step1ne's AI interviewer first conducts an initial interview (this part is done by AI, and you will be told beforehand). A consultant then compiles the results and screens your résumé; if you pass, we arrange an in-person interview in Taipei where you meet the hiring company directly.

I'm currently employed. Will my current company find out that I applied?+

No. Step1ne will never send your information without your consent, and will not contact your current employer. Interviews can be scheduled after your working hours.
