Job description

Supply chain strategy and integration

  • Set the group's supply chain strategy, connecting demand forecasting, purchasing, production coordination, inventory, and logistics
  • Integrate supply and delivery across the Cambodia and Philippines plants and US private-label partner factories
  • Improve delivery efficiency and optimize overall cost while balancing revenue, gross margin, cost and inventory targets

Procurement and supplier management

  • Lead supplier development, evaluation, and contract negotiations
  • Develop procurement strategies to ensure the quality, lead times, and stable supply of raw materials and products
  • Build supplier performance management and risk backup mechanisms

Overseas markets and key accounts

  • Expand overseas markets, key accounts, and channels, and set overseas market development strategy
  • Lead quoting and commercial negotiations, and own revenue and gross margin targets
  • Analyze market trends, identify new business opportunities and maintain relationships with international customers

Cross-border orders and risk control

  • Oversee import/export and cross-border order fulfillment, and arrange cross-border logistics
  • Work with finance, legal, and other departments to manage trade compliance, customer credit, and collection risk

Team leadership and operations analysis

  • Lead domestic and overseas teams (about 10 people at first, covering supply chain and export sales), driving cross-functional collaboration, performance management, and process improvement
  • Track costs, lead times, and inventory with operational data, and regularly present operational analysis and strategic recommendations to the CEO / Chairman

Group strategic projects and digital transformation

  • Carry out cross-border and strategic projects assigned by the general manager
  • Take part in digitizing the group's supply chain: automating processes such as load planning, freight rate comparison, and shipment tracking, plus a supply chain data dashboard (a direction disclosed in the annual report)
  • The group uses Digiwin ERP and continues to drive AI and digital adoption

Requirements

Required
  • Bachelor's degree or above
  • 15+ years in the textile and apparel industry, including at least 5 years at manager level or above
  • A track record in supply chain integration and international trade / overseas sales management, able to manage revenue, gross margin, cost and inventory at the same time
  • Familiar with international business negotiation, import/export, and cross-border logistics
  • Experience leading multinational teams, with strong cross-functional integration skills
  • Fluent in Chinese and English; able to run meetings, negotiate and review business documents in English independently (TOEIC 750+)
  • Familiar with AI tools and applications and Office software; able to work independently
Preferred qualifications
  • Experience in medical devices, medical consumables, protective equipment or textile manufacturing
  • Have managed overseas production bases or a multi-site supply chain
  • Have led a supply chain transformation or been responsible for a business unit's P&L
  • Familiar with ERP systems and supply chain digitalization; OEM/ODM or B2B sales experience
  • A second foreign language (Japanese preferred)

FAQ

Who will I be leading when I start?+

You will start by leading about 10 people in supply chain and export sales, then gradually expand to domestic and overseas teams of about 15–20.

Is this role mainly supply chain, or does it also carry sales targets?+

Both. The scope covers supply chain, procurement, overseas business development and projects assigned by the general manager, and you are accountable for revenue and gross margin targets.

How much English will I need?+

English is required: you need to chair international meetings and handle business negotiations on your own (TOEIC 750+). A second foreign language is a plus, especially Japanese.

Does the group have offices in the Americas?+

It has OEM partner factories in the US, with production bases in Cambodia and the Philippines.

I am currently employed. Will my current employer find out that I applied?+

You can start with a confidential conversation with a Step1ne consultant and decide whether to enter the formal process after learning about the opportunity. Without your consent, your consultant will never send your résumé to a company or reveal anything about your job search to your current employer.

Is a long-term overseas assignment required?+

No. The job is based at the Taipei headquarters; overseas trips are mainly short, generally no more than one month in total per year.

Is a textile and apparel background required?+

Yes. This role requires 15+ years in the textile and apparel industry; related manufacturing experience in medical consumables or protective equipment is a further plus.

What does the interview process look like?+

After passing the first conversation with your consultant, you will interview with the HR manager and then the general manager, and if needed with the group's top executive.
