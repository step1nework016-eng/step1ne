FAQ

This is a staffing position. Are pay and insurance less secure?+

No. During the dispatch period, Step1ne signs a formal employment contract with you, and your pay, labor and health insurance, annual leave, and other rights are all handled under the Labor Standards Act, the same as any other employee.

Day to day, do I work at Step1ne or at the hiring company?+

Day-to-day work takes place on-site at the host company (a listed/OTC-listed gaming group), where an on-site supervisor assigns your actual tasks.

How many interview rounds are there?+

There are two rounds, a first and a second interview. Before scheduling, your Step1ne consultant will walk you through what to focus on and what to watch out for.

I heard this role has a test — what's on it?+

This job does include a written test, but the exact format and time limit are not yet confirmed; your consultant will explain before the interview.
