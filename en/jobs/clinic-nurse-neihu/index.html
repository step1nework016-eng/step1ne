Rehabilitation Clinic NurseA rehabilitation clinic in Neihu, Taipei is hiring a full-time permanent nurse. Starting salary NT$43,000–50,000, no specific education or experience required. Duties include assisting doctors during consultations, drawing blood and assisting with injections, with one-on-one training from your supervisor

Three highlights of this opportunity: ① No cap on starting pay and no reduction based on education or experience — it falls within NT$43,000–50,000 ② Three types of bonuses (blood draws / injections / patient volume), which you share in from your fourth month after completing three months on the job ③ One-on-one training from your supervisor, so you can build solid skills even without a rehabilitation background

Job description

Clinical assistance

  • Assisting at consultations
  • Blood draws
  • Pre-injection preparation (prolotherapy, PRP, etc.)
  • Ultrasound exam assistance

Your supervisor teaches all of the above personally, so you can pick it up even without rehabilitation experience.

Patient education and environment

  • Basic health education
  • Basic cleaning

Other

  • Tasks assigned by your manager

Requirements

Required
  • Nursing license
  • Able to work day, mid, and night shifts (rotating)
Preferred qualifications
  • Familiarity with prolotherapy and PRP injection procedures
  • Rehabilitation or clinic experience preferred

Why this opportunity

No cap on starting pay—set by experience

Starting pay is not limited by education or work experience; the actual figure is set within NT$43,000–50,000 based on your background and performance after joining.

Three bonus schemes — the more you do, the more you earn

There are also blood-draw, injection and patient-volume bonuses; you join the bonus pool from your fourth month, after three months on the job, so your performance shows directly in your pay.

Your manager trains you personally, so your learning is well supported

The clinic has about 10 people, and new hires are trained personally by the manager, so you can learn step by step even without a rehabilitation background.

No education or experience requirements — a simple bar

All you need is a nursing license to get started. No rehabilitation or clinic experience? You are still welcome to apply — those with related experience will simply get up to speed faster.

FAQ

Roughly how much are the bonuses?+

There are also three types of bonuses for blood draws, injections, and patient volume. Actual amounts are calculated by clinic policy and the month's case count, and exact figures are explained at the interview.

Can I apply without rehabilitation department experience?+

Yes. Education and work experience are not restricted; a nursing license is all you need to start the conversation. Experience in rehabilitation departments or clinics will help you get up to speed faster.

Can I have a fixed shift?+

The current schedule is rotating shifts with scheduled days off (day / mid / evening). We recommend confirming exactly how shifts are scheduled directly with the clinic at the interview.
