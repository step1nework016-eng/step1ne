Engineering Design Engineer (Trainee Program)Hiring newcomers for semiconductor plant engineering design—senior colleagues will teach you starting from on-site practice

Job Requirements at a Glance

* The above terms are current plans; actual compensation and working conditions will be as confirmed by both parties after the interview.

Job description

Engineering drafting

  • Produce CAD drawings for the various systems in high-tech plant projects

On-site collaboration and project support

  • Assist with site surveys to make sure drawings match the site
  • Complete tasks assigned by your supervisor in line with company project progress

Requirements

Required
  • Basic drafting skills in AutoCAD or Revit and a strong eagerness to learn
  • Able to read civil, architectural, MEP or plumbing and electrical drawings
  • A standard heavy motorcycle license for daily commuting (dormitories are provided for staff from other cities and counties, usually not far from the site)
  • Associate degree or above in industrial design, civil engineering (including construction), or architecture/interior design
Preferred qualifications
  • AutoCAD or Revit international certification
  • Experience with construction tendering, shop drawings or as-built drawings

Why this opportunity

Mentored, learning from real work

No minimum experience. New graduates and career changers from related majors or with basic drafting skills are welcome. Your manager will assign 1–2 senior colleagues to guide you as you learn hands-on at the construction site, while getting familiar with the company's purchase requests, daily construction reports, acceptance inspections, and other processes.

Flexible pay that recognizes your effort

NT$35,000–45,000 per month, based on your past experience, professional skills and interview performance. Candidates with backup-related certifications or hands-on experience are offered above-market pay, and raises after joining are based on performance.

Worry-free remote site assignments

Staff from other cities get employee housing, plus a monthly trip-home allowance and fuel allowance for project assignments, so you can work on site with peace of mind.

Multiple bonuses, real rewards

Year-end bonus, mid-year bonus, Dragon Boat and Mid-Autumn Festival bonuses, Labor Day gift money, birthday gift money, plus referral bonuses for recommending great colleagues, so your hard work gets noticed.

Ride the semiconductor construction boom

The company specializes in new construction and expansion of semiconductor and high-tech plants, with projects across Taiwan — joining the team means taking part directly in building Taiwan's semiconductor industry.
