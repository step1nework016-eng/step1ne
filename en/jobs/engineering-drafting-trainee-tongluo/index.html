FAQ

What is the employment type?+

Mainly one-year project contracts, with some staffing positions; the project is expected to last about one year. Strong performers may be considered for conversion to full-time by the hiring company.

Is housing provided?+

Yes. You choose between housing and a transport allowance: if you live in the dorm, you also get round-trip Tze-Chiang express train fares home for 4 weeks a month; if you commute instead, you get a fuel allowance based on days worked.

Is it okay if I have no engineering drafting experience?+

Yes, no experience is needed — AutoCAD skills are enough, and the company provides training. Revit skills or an AutoCAD certification are a plus.

Which company is it?+

The hiring unit prefers not to disclose its name at the initial contact stage. Once you pass the initial assessment, your consultant will fully explain the company background and project status when they get in touch.
