Executive Assistant (based in the Hakuba resort area, Nagano, Japan)Hiring for a Japanese resort operator: a core aide supporting the owner and senior executives directly (an individual-contributor role with no team-management responsibilities)

Based full-time in the Hakuba resort area, Nagano. Company housing and a company car for getting around and driving duties are provided, so you can settle in as soon as you arrive—no commuting back and forth between Taiwan and Japan.

Job description

Translation and document work

  • Chinese–Japanese / English–Japanese interpreting and translation
  • Assisting with Japanese business documents

Scheduling and travel arrangements

  • Plan and manage the executive's schedule and accompany them throughout as needed

Communication and coordination

  • Serve as the executive's main point of contact and coordinator with internal teams and outside parties
  • Arrange meetings and send notices, write minutes and follow up on decisions

Project support

  • Support your manager with assigned tasks and project work

Requirements

Required
  • 2+ years of experience as an assistant, secretary, interpreter/translator, or in executive support
  • Japanese at JLPT N1 or N2 level, able to communicate in real time in meetings and handle business documents
  • Ability to communicate in Chinese or English
  • Bachelor's degree or above
  • Able to provide a police criminal record certificate (certificate of good conduct)
  • Signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is required
  • A visa / residence status that allows you to work legally in Japan (if you need to apply, the company can assist with the application, but approval is not guaranteed)
Preferred qualifications
  • Driving experience in Japan and familiarity with local traffic
  • Strong English skills
  • Experience in hotels, food and beverage, or resort operations

Why this opportunity

Report directly to the owner and senior executives

An individual-contributor role with no management responsibility, giving you a close-up view of the company's decision-making core and rare experience as a senior aide.

Fixed annual salary of JPY 4,200,000

Monthly salary of JPY 350,000, with the possibility of a year-end bonus (twice a year, not guaranteed) depending on company performance and individual results—a stable income base.

Full Japanese social insurance and living support

Health insurance, employees' pension, employment insurance and workers' accident insurance are provided under Japanese law; company housing is provided while stationed in Hakuba, Nagano. Car benefit: a company car is also provided for daily transport and driving duties.

Frontline work experience at a Japanese resort

You will be based mainly in the Hakuba resort area of Nagano. During the winter peak season you will experience resort operations up close, working on the front line of Japan's resort industry in a way that is rare in Taiwan.

FAQ

Does this role involve going back and forth between Taiwan and Japan, or is it based in Japan full-time?+

This position is based in Japan full-time, not split between Taiwan and Japan. The contractual workplace is the Tokyo head office, but you will mainly be stationed in Hakuba, Nagano Prefecture. You arrange your own housing in Tokyo; company housing is provided in Hakuba. We recommend weighing the cost of living in Japan and your willingness to live there long-term before applying.

Is Japanese really a hard requirement?+

Yes, Japanese at JLPT N1 or N2 level is required, and the interview process will include a conversation in Japanese to verify it.

Will my current employer find out that I applied?+

No. Step1ne recruits confidentially. Consultants never send your résumé to a company without your consent and never disclose your application to your current employer.

Does the company cover flights to Japan or back to Taiwan?+

There is currently no subsidy for flights to Japan or back to Taiwan; please factor this in before applying.

Is Step1ne a licensed headhunting and recruitment agency?+

Step1ne is a brand of 德仁管理顧問有限公司 (Taiwan Uniform Business No. 85046127). It holds Taipei City employment services license No. 0363 and focuses on executive search, full-time recruitment, and temporary staffing services.

Will exchange-rate fluctuations affect my salary when converted to NT$?+

Yes. Salary is set and paid directly in yen, and there is currently no exchange-rate compensation or NT$ subsidy. We recommend factoring exchange-rate fluctuations into your overall financial planning before applying.
