Finance ControllerJoin a multinational medical supplies manufacturing group and run the full finance function of its Cambodia site on your own, with a monthly salary of US$3,000–4,000 after tax plus an expatriate allowance, on a path toward group CFO

Job Requirements at a Glance

Salary & compensation
Monthly salary US$3,000–4,000 (net, after tax)Overseas allowance paid separately, plus an annual performance bonus; negotiable based on experience
Nature of the role
Full-time · Long-term overseas posting
Location
Cambodia (a special economic zone near Phnom Penh)
Reports to
CFO, General Manager / President
Leading the team
Local finance and accounting team of about 5–10 people

* The above terms are current plans; actual compensation and working conditions will be as confirmed by both parties after the interview.

Job description

Financial management

  • Monthly, quarterly and annual closing and financial statement preparation
  • Ensure operations comply with local regulations and group accounting policies
  • Provide management reports and financial analysis to support business decisions

Cost management

  • Factory cost management, cost accounting, and inventory management
  • Analyze costs and propose gross margin improvements and cost optimization
  • Work with the plant operations team to improve operational efficiency

Tax and regulatory compliance

  • Local tax filing and management
  • Support external auditors with audits and financial attestation
  • Maintain relationships with government agencies, accountants, and tax advisors

Team management

  • Manage and develop the local finance team
  • Build financial systems and talent development mechanisms
  • Work closely with Taiwan headquarters and the regional management team

Requirements

Required
  • 10+ years of finance/accounting experience, including 5+ years in financial management
  • Plant finance experience in manufacturing, with solid knowledge of cost accounting and factory cost analysis
  • Experience using ERP systems
  • Open to long-term overseas assignment
Preferred qualifications
  • Overseas work or expatriate experience, ideally in Southeast Asia
  • Experience in textiles, apparel, or other manufacturing
  • Previous experience as a Finance Controller, Finance Manager, Plant Finance Manager, or Factory Controller
  • Experience reporting to an overseas regional or group headquarters, and familiarity with how multinational companies are managed

Why this opportunity

A development path toward group CFO

This role independently runs the entire finance function of the local company. It is not a narrowly scoped task role under headquarters, but a development position the group intends to groom toward CFO.

Competitive pay

The net monthly salary of USD 3,000–4,000 is take-home pay after tax, with an overseas allowance paid separately; the annual total comes to roughly NT$1–2 million, negotiable based on experience, plus an annual performance bonus based on individual and plant performance.

Full support for overseas assignments

In addition to the expatriate allowance, housing and transportation subsidies are provided, along with home leave and flexible annual leave. Details will be explained at the interview stage based on your personal and family situation, so that long-term life abroad feels more comfortable.

Lead both people and systems

You will directly lead a local finance and accounting team of 5–10 people, personally managing and training local staff while building the financial system: a role where you can truly put your management and system-building skills to work.

Confidential from start to finish

Your consultant will not send your resume to any employer or tell your current employer anything without your consent, so you can evaluate this opportunity with peace of mind while still employed.
