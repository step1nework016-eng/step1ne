Job description

Taking requirements and finding talent

  • Take on client hiring needs and turn vague expectations into an actionable candidate profile
  • Proactively find and reach out to candidates, including first contact by phone and message

Advancing and closing

  • Scheduling interviews, preparing recommendation materials, and confirming details back and forth with clients
  • Negotiate salary and offers, and bridge gaps in expectations on both sides

FAQ

Can I apply with no headhunting experience at all?+

Yes—this role doesn't require related experience in the first place. We have ready-made search strategies and scripts, and new hires get mentoring early on. What you really need is a willingness to reach out to strangers and comfort with a relatively long sales cycle.

How are bonuses calculated?+

Closed deals are paid on commission: the more you close, the higher your bonus, with no cap. The rate is adjusted by tenure and deal type. Because every deal and every person moves at a different pace, we explain the actual numbers for your situation in the interview rather than giving a range up front that may not be accurate.

How long until my first closed deal?+

A headhunting placement cycle usually takes 2–3 months, and a newcomer's first placement usually takes longer. This is the one thing to think through before taking this job: for the first few months, you will likely keep working without results yet.

Will I have to make cold calls all the time?+

Yes, and it is one of the core parts of the job. You do not need to be naturally unafraid of rejection, but you do need to be willing to make the call even when you are nervous.

Where is the job, and what is the work arrangement?+

Taipei City, with client visits. On-site vs. remote arrangements will be explained at the interview.
