Job Requirements at a Glance

* The above terms are current plans; actual compensation and working conditions will be as confirmed by both parties after the interview.

Job description

Technical training and growth

  • The company provides basic AutoCAD training covering 2D drafting, revisions and reading engineering drawings
  • After learning AutoCAD basics, move on to Revit 3D modeling

Requirements

Required
  • Bachelor's degree or above, any major (related majors preferred)
  • Comfortable communicating in Chinese
  • A motorcycle license
  • Able to work on-site at semiconductor fab sites in Hsinchu and attend occupational safety training
  • A mature, steady work attitude, able to handle a degree of pressure, and willing to undergo training and follow team and project assignments
Preferred qualifications
  • Basic AutoCAD experience
  • Some exposure to Revit or other 3D modeling tools
  • Able to read engineering drawings
  • Interested in the semiconductor industry, MEP systems and BIM technology
  • Background in electrical, mechanical (including HVAC and energy), industrial design, civil (including construction), architecture, or related fields

Why this opportunity

BIM training from zero

The company provides basic AutoCAD training, and once you are comfortable with 2D drawings you move on to Revit 3D modeling. No prior software experience is required — it is a practical starting point for entering BIM and facility engineering.

Work on world-class semiconductor facility projects

Work directly on a facilities engineering project at a major Hsinchu semiconductor manufacturer and gain front-line experience building BIM for fab MEP equipment and piping.

Stable pay, set according to education

NT$40,833–50,167/month (bachelor's pay band; the master's pay band is set separately based on qualifications), with a 14-month annual salary paid out evenly across the year and an annual review with a chance for a raise.

Regular hours, two days off a week, no shifts

Regular hours of 08:30–17:30 with a one-hour lunch break, two days off per week and no shifts. Overtime is paid in full according to the Labor Standards Act.

Hiring 10 urgently — plenty of openings now

This project urgently needs 10 people and is expanding at the same time, so now is a good time to apply with a higher chance of being hired.

FAQ

Is this a staffing role or a permanent position?+

This is a permanent position: once hired, you're directly a permanent employee of the hiring company — not staffing and not a contract role.

How much overtime is there?+

Overtime is required during projects, with actual hours depending on the project workload; overtime pay follows the Labor Standards Act and is paid in full for actual hours worked, calculated on your full salary.

How is the monthly salary calculated?+

Pay is a 14-month annual salary spread evenly over 12 months (base starting salary × 14 ÷ 12). The advertised monthly range of NT$40,833–50,167 applies to bachelor's degree holders; the range for master's degree holders is set separately based on qualifications. Salary is reviewed annually based on performance.

How soon can I start after being hired? What do I need to prepare?+

After hiring, you will complete about 6 hours of occupational safety training, including the construction industry "Little Green Card" course required for site entry. After that, you will report to a major semiconductor fab project in Hsinchu as arranged by the company.
