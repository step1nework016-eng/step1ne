Job description

Web development and faithful design implementation

  • Work with the operations unit on web development, content updates, and maintenance
  • Faithfully turn design mockups into working web pages

Animation Effects & Teamwork

  • Build web motion effects to improve the interactive user experience
  • Coordinate with operations, the backend team, and designers to keep projects moving smoothly

Requirements

Required
  • 3+ years of front-end development experience
  • A portfolio demonstrating hands-on skills is required (personal projects, freelance work or company work all count)
  • Skilled at building interactive effects in JavaScript
  • Strong CSS/SASS and responsive (RWD) development skills
  • Able to accurately slice and build pages from Photoshop designs
  • Able to troubleshoot cross-browser compatibility issues
Preferred qualifications
  • Experience with Git version control and CI/CD
  • Familiar with AJAX and backend API integration
  • Experience with front-end frameworks (Vue, Pinia, Vuex, mainly via CDN)
  • Good at working with AI and used to AI IDEs

Why this opportunity

Your work goes live, so you can see the impact

What you build goes live in products run by a listed gaming group. Every line of code you write and every animation you create will be seen and used by a large number of players. This is not a project that ends on a shelf.

Salary based on ability, with room to negotiate

Salary is negotiable. A consultant will first learn your expected salary and then negotiate with the hiring company, so that your technical skills and experience are fairly reflected in your package rather than fitted to a standard pay scale.

Staffing ≠ fewer rights

During the assignment Step1ne signs a formal employment contract with you, pays your salary and enrolls you in labor and health insurance. Annual leave and other rights follow the Labor Standards Act in full, the same as any other employee.

A consultant negotiates alongside you the whole way; it doesn't end once your résumé is sent

A Step1ne consultant first holds a confidential initial conversation to understand your situation and requirements, and only submits your profile to a company to arrange interviews once we confirm it is a good fit. Throughout the process, someone is looking out for you.

FAQ

Is this role posted by a recruitment agency? Who is the company behind it?+

Step1ne is a brand of 德仁管理顧問有限公司 (Tax ID 85046127). It holds an Employment Service License issued by the Taipei City Government (Taipei City Employment Service License No. 0363), with its registered address at 3F, No. 54-7, Sec. 3, Kangning Rd., Neihu Dist., Taipei City, and focuses on executive search, permanent recruitment and staffing services.

Are staffing jobs worse than permanent jobs in terms of rights and benefits?+

No. During the dispatch period, Step1ne signs a formal employment contract with you, and your pay, labor and health insurance, annual leave, and other rights are all handled under the Labor Standards Act, the same as any other employee.

Do I have to include a portfolio when applying?+

Yes. This role places special weight on accurate layout implementation and building motion effects, so you need to provide a portfolio (personal projects, freelance work, or company work all count) to show your skills.

How does the interview process work?+

There are three stages: first an initial interview with A-Cai, our AI interviewer; then a confidential conversation with a consultant about your situation and expectations; and if it's a good fit, a formal interview and offer discussion. The hiring company makes the final decision.
