Job description

VIP guest hospitality

  • Host VIP guests and deliver attentive, warm, high-end service that makes them feel at home
  • Quickly grasp and properly handle on-site incidents and guest needs

VIP relationship management

  • Maintain VIP relationships and build long-term trust and rapport

On-site and inventory management

  • Keep the premises clean and refined, and make sure facilities and equipment work properly
  • Manage spare parts and resource inventory systematically and help the team complete daily operations

Requirements

Required
  • Good communication and emotional management skills
  • Strong sense of responsibility and empathy, with respect for guest privacy and confidentiality
  • Basic Office skills (Word/Excel) for keeping supply and inventory records
  • Able to provide a Police Criminal Record Certificate (good-conduct certificate)
Preferred qualifications
  • Experience in related hospitality work such as guest houses or hotels will help you pick up the pace of guest service faster
