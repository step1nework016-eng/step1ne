AI Customer Success Manager (Business Development + Long-term Account Management)AI startup · Full-time · Nangang Software Park

Online stores spend on ads every month to bring customers in, but there is no one in the store to greet them; when customers have questions, no one answers, and they browse and then buy elsewhere. This startup builds that “sales associate” — an AI salesperson installed on e-commerce platforms. This role is responsible for acquiring and growing long-term relationships with large merchants: finding merchants, closing contracts, and making sure the product truly delivers results in their stores. You will be one of the very first members of the sales team.

Job description

Prospect and win large e-commerce merchants

  • You own the whole sales cycle, from first contact to signing the contract

Help merchants go live

  • Get to know each merchant's products in depth, how they handle returns and exchanges, and how far they can go on discounts
  • Define the AI sales agent's permission scope together with the client

Keep nurturing client relationships after go-live

  • Take ownership of any issue a merchant runs into
  • When product or engineering needs to step in, you connect the right people and follow through to resolution

Be the merchant's voice inside the company

  • Bring issues back to the product team
  • Organize it to the point where the team can act on it directly

Requirements

Required
  • 2+ years of work experience in any industry—e-commerce, finance, healthcare, legal, sales, consulting, marketing, software engineering and more are all welcome
  • A deep understanding of what real work looks like in a particular field
  • Fluent written and spoken English for working with merchants and colleagues across time zones

Why this opportunity

One of the earliest members of the sales team

Which merchants to target and what case to make to win them over are things you help define — you are not inheriting someone else's script.

Full ownership, not just the first half

Prospecting, signing, onboarding and long-term account management are all yours, so you get to see how the merchants you brought in do over time.

Any background

The team believes great CSMs come from all kinds of backgrounds—what matters is how deeply you understand what real work looks like in a given field.

A big market worth going deep in

The US market alone has nearly 2.7 million mid-to-large e-commerce merchants, and the number keeps growing. You will mainly work with mid-to-large brand merchants with high order values and stable partnerships, so a single deal usually turns into a long-term relationship. The cross-border B2B SaaS sales and e-commerce ecosystem experience you build here is directly transferable to any international SaaS company later on.

FAQ

Roughly what is the pay?+

The employer lists pay as negotiable (regular monthly pay of NT$40,000 or more); the actual amount depends on your experience and interview results.

Can I apply without e-commerce or SaaS experience?+

Yes. The requirement is 2+ years of work experience in any industry — e-commerce, finance, healthcare, law, sales, consulting, marketing and software engineering are all welcome.

What level of English is required?+

Both written and spoken fluency are required, since you'll work with merchants and colleagues across time zones. This will be checked in the interview.

Which company is it?+

The employer prefers not to disclose its name at the initial stage. Once you pass the first assessment, the consultant will fully explain the company's background, products, and team when they contact you.
