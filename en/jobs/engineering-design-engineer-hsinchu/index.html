Senior Engineering Design EngineerHigh-tech plant construction projects · Zhubei, Hsinchu / Taoyuan–Hsinchu–Miaoli / Taichung / Yunlin · AutoCAD/Revit

Based mainly in Zhubei, Hsinchu, with possible assignments to Taoyuan/Hsinchu/Miaoli, Taichung, or Yunlin fab sites depending on the project. Dormitory housing for out-of-area assignments is fully covered by the company, and you won't pay for AutoCAD / Revit international certification training either. The hiring company specializes in design and construction management for new builds and expansions of high-tech fabs and laboratories.

Job description

Engineering Drafting and Design

  • Engineering CAD drawings for each system in high-tech plants
  • Complete tasks assigned by your supervisor according to project progress

On-site collaboration

  • Assist with site surveys to make sure drawings match the site

Requirements

Required
  • 3+ years of engineering design experience
  • Associate degree or above, any major
  • Advanced AutoCAD and Revit drafting skills
  • Experience in plant or engineering design, with an understanding of the related knowledge and how it fits together
  • A standard heavy motorcycle license for daily commuting
Preferred qualifications
  • AutoCAD international certification
  • Revit international certification

Why this opportunity

Fully provided housing — no need to find your own place

When assigned to out-of-area projects, the company fully covers dormitory housing—no rent or utilities out of your pocket.

Commuting costs subsidized

Fuel subsidies are provided for commuting from nearby cities and counties, plus a home-visit travel allowance for project assignments.

Certification costs paid by the company

Training and courses such as AutoCAD and Revit international certifications are fully covered, at no cost to you.

Your drawings go straight into real plants

You will draw real engineering drawings for semiconductor and high-tech facilities, not practice sheets, and help with site surveys to confirm the drawings match the site.

FAQ

Do I have to know Revit?+

Yes. This role requires advanced AutoCAD and Revit skills, which you'll use in practice for systems engineering drawings at high-tech fabs.

Will I need to visit construction sites often?+

The main work is drafting and design, but you will also help with site surveys to make sure drawings match the site.

Can I apply without an international certification?+

Certifications are a plus, not a requirement. The main bar is hands-on AutoCAD / Revit skills and plant design experience. The company also fully subsidizes certification training.

Is there a lot of overtime?+

Mainly day shifts (08:30–17:30). Overtime depends on actual project progress and is paid by the company in accordance with the law; details will be explained at the interview.
