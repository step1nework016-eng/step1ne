Senior Test Product Specialist (Games · Neihu, Taipei)Temporary staffing · Full-time | 40–45K per month | Mon–Fri · two days off a week | Functional testing / Bug tracking / Test report writing

The title says "senior," but this time the hiring team hasn't set a minimum years of experience. If you've done functional testing, tracked bugs through to closure, and written your own test plans and test reports, it's worth applying. The role is at a listed group's office in Neihu, Taipei, and covers quality gatekeeping before and after game product launches: writing and maintaining test cases, running user, functional and stress tests on schedule, logging reproduction steps when you find an issue and following it until it's fixed, then compiling the results into test reports for the product and development teams. It's more than just clicking around — you write the test plans and reports yourself, and you take the initiative to clarify cross-team issues. Fixed schedule, Monday to Friday; 1 opening. During the staffing assignment you sign a formal employment contract with Step1ne (德仁管理顧問有限公司), and your day-to-day work takes place at the hiring company's Neihu office.

Job description

Test cases and test plans

  • Write game test cases from product specs and maintain existing cases as versions update
  • Plan test scope and schedules, and write test plans

Run tests

  • Run scheduled regular tests on features, including user testing, functional testing, and stress testing
  • Run regression tests after each version update to confirm existing features have not been broken

Issue tracking and reporting

  • Record reproduction steps and impact scope for issues, classify their severity, and enter them into the bug tracking process
  • Track issue fixes through to closure and complete verification
  • Compile test results, write test reports, and submit them to the product and development teams

Cross-department coordination

  • Help the product and development teams pin down root causes and gaps in how specs are interpreted
  • Coordinate testing schedules and fix priorities with other departments (including product and development leads)

Requirements

Required
  • Hands-on functional testing experience
  • Familiar with bug tracking: able to fully document, prioritize, and follow issues through to closure
  • Software integration testing experience
  • Able to write test plans and test reports independently
  • Able to communicate with product, development, and other roles, explain problems clearly, and drive them to resolution
  • Able to work Monday to Friday at the Neihu, Taipei office
Preferred qualifications
  • Product testing experience at the source code level (able to read code to help pinpoint root causes)
  • You play Japanese games regularly and know how these products work and how users behave
  • Experience with test case management or bug tracking systems
