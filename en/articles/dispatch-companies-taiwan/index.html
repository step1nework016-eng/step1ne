Staffing agencies in Taiwan: comparing multinational, large local, regional and specialist agencies

By the Step1ne executive search consultant team Published 2026-10-19 About 8 min read
Short answer

Staffing agencies in Taiwan fall roughly into four types: multinational full-service firms (e.g. Adecco), large local firms (e.g. 有根, IMC, 汎亞), regional temp agencies (e.g. 吉陞 in Kaohsiung, 金好用 in Taichung), and specialist/consulting firms (e.g. 芮可, or consulting firms that also handle permanent hiring). None of them publish their management fees on their websites, so the point of comparison is not who is cheapest but what types of roles they staff, which regions they serve, whether they also take on outsourced contracts, and how their quotes are broken down.

3Key points of each firm

Adecco

A multinational HR services group headquartered in Switzerland, established in Taiwan in 1989. Its staffing covers short-term staffing (peak seasons, event staff), contract staffing and professional staffing, plus outsourcing services for sales and marketing, HR, reception, customer service, and warehousing and logistics.

有根人力

A Taiwanese firm founded in 1994. Its website states that it provides temporary staffing and contracted operations, with contracted work including processing lines and assembly, packaging, warehousing and logistics, and cold-chain storage, and it lists certifications such as ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

IMC (精英 / 精準)

A Taiwanese integrated human resources group founded in 1991, with offices across northern, central, and southern Taiwan. Its staffing services are split into long-term contract staff and temporary staff, and it also offers professional staffing, payroll outsourcing, and project outsourcing. The footer of its website lists private employment service license No. 2955 (精英) and No. 2204 (精準).

汎亞人力派遣

A staffing brand with 6 locations across northern, central and southern Taiwan. Its services include staffing (recruiting, labor and health insurance, payroll calculation and payment), recruitment on behalf of employers (hires become the company's full-time employees), and payroll management.

芮可企管顧問

Founded in 2004 in Tainan, its core business is talent assessment and training; it also bundles outsourced contracting, short-term services, and event support as “hybrid staffing”—for example, administrative, customer service, warehouse stocktaking, and event guide staff.

吉陞人力 (Jisheng Staffing)

Founded in 2015 and centered in Kaohsiung, with service offices in Taichung and Taoyuan. Its core business is temporary and day labor and warehouse picking, and it also offers contracted services, warehouse management, and processing and packaging.

金好用人力

Specializes in temporary workers, general laborers, day labor and skilled day trades such as electrical and plumbing in the greater Taichung area — suited to short-term or temporary gaps.

5FAQ

Do staffing agencies need an employment services license?+

Under Articles 34 and 35 of Taiwan's Employment Service Act, anyone doing job placement, labor brokerage, or recruiting employees on commission must first obtain a private employment service agency license. “Labor dispatch” is not among the businesses listed in those articles; staffing is governed by the Labor Standards Act. The official materials we found don't answer this question directly, so the above is our reading of the statutes. In practice, many staffing agencies also recruit for permanent roles and therefore hold the license as well; before signing, you can ask which licenses they hold.

Roughly how much is a staffing agency's management fee?+

None of the 7 firms compared in this article publish their management fees on their websites. When requesting a quote, ask them to list employee salary, statutory employer costs, and the management fee separately so you can compare the difference. For how to calculate it, see How staffing fees are calculated.

What's the difference between staffing and contracting out?+

With staffing, people employed by the staffing agency work at your company under your direction and supervision. With contracting, you hand a whole block of work to the other party to complete, and they manage their own people. Which to choose depends on whether you need "people" or "finished work."

Can temp workers be converted to permanent employees later?+

Yes, but it must follow the contract between the two parties; the usual approach is to agree in advance on the conditions and fee for conversion to permanent employment. Interviewing and selecting someone first and then asking the agency to employ them carries legal risk; see Legal pitfalls for companies using staffing.
