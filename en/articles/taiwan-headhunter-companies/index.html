Headhunting firms in Taiwan: 104 Headhunting, Cake, 1111's 愛客獵, and international vs. local headhunters compared

By the Step1ne executive search consultant team Published 2026-10-19 About 8 min read
Short answer

Headhunting services commonly seen in Taiwan fall into roughly four types: services attached to job banks or job platforms (104 Headhunting, 1111's 愛客獵 service, Cake Headhunting), international headhunters (Adecco, Michael Page, Robert Walters), platform models (HuntByte, where multiple freelance consultants recommend candidates together), and independent headhunters based in Taiwan (such as Step1ne). Each firm's published fee structure differs: 104 Headhunting states 20%–35% of annual salary, while most international firms don't publish their rates. Which one to choose mainly depends on the role, your budget, and how you want to work together.

3Key points of each firm

104 獵才 (104's headhunting service)

Operated by 104 Corporation (一零四資訊科技), it centers on 104's talent database and covers roles from general manager, vice president, and CFO down to manager level and specialists, for companies of any size. Its website states fees of 20%–35% depending on annual salary tier, charged only upon hire, and it also offers a “Key Talent Service” that lets companies search the talent pool themselves. According to its website, the most placements in 2025 were in electronics, ICT, and semiconductors.

Accurate (愛客獵)

According to its website, it grew out of the executive search center of the group behind 1111 (a Taiwanese job site) and became an independent brand in 2021. It serves executives, mid- to senior-level managers, and professionals, with an emphasis on confidential hiring. Its FAQ states that if a candidate leaves during probation, it will recommend a replacement free of charge.

Cake headhunting

The headhunting service offered by the job platform Cake, covering roles from junior to mid/senior level, with strengths in IT, semiconductors, electronics, and finance. Its website states that fees are charged only after the hire starts and that there is a guarantee period, with details depending on the service contract; it also offers high-volume hiring, staffing, and Employer of Record (EoR) services.

HuntByte

Its website describes itself as "not another headhunting firm" but a platform where multiple freelance headhunting consultants and referrers recommend candidates together; the company deals with a single point of contact, and the role is opened to the platform's consultants to compete in referring candidates. Suited to mid-to-senior, scarce or long-open roles.

Adecco

A multinational HR group headquartered in Switzerland, established in Taiwan in 1989, with branches in Taipei, Hsinchu, Taichung, Tainan, and Kaohsiung. Its permanent recruitment is split into executive, professional, and high-volume hiring, and it also offers staffing, outsourcing, and training.

Michael Page

A multinational recruitment firm offering permanent, contract and executive search placements in Taiwan, plus executive assessment, board search and compensation consulting. Its specialist functions cover accounting and finance, engineering and manufacturing, technology, sales, HR and more.

Robert Walters

Entered the Taiwan market in 2011, offering permanent recruitment, executive search and recruitment process outsourcing (RPO). Its website states that each client is assigned a consultant familiar with its industry, with specialties including semiconductors, electronics and industrial, software, and accounting and finance.

5FAQ

Is there a ranking of headhunting firms in Taiwan?+

There is no official ranking of headhunting firms. The closest official reference is the evaluation grade (e.g. Grade A) that Taiwan's competent authority gives private employment service agencies; you can ask a firm for its evaluation results and license number to verify. Rankings online are mostly compiled by individual writers, with inconsistent standards.

How does 104's headhunting service charge?+

According to the official site of 104's headhunting service, fees are 20%–35% depending on the annual salary level, charged only after the candidate successfully starts; the actual percentage is negotiated case by case.

What's the difference between local Taiwanese and multinational headhunting firms?+

Multinational search firms have offices in many countries and suit foreign companies and cross-border roles; local Taiwanese firms know how Taiwanese companies make decisions and their culture, and usually have the same consultant handle the search directly. Either way, check whether the consultant actually assigned to you knows your industry.

Can I engage several headhunters at the same time?+

Yes, unless you've signed an exclusive agreement. If you use several at once, set rules in the contract for who gets credit for duplicate referrals; the common practice is that the firm that referred the candidate first, with a written record, takes precedence.
