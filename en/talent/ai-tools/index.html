AI Career Toolkit

AI job search tools: type keywords on your phone to build a resume, practice interviews, and write a resignation letter

Short answer:The Step1ne Referrals LINE mini app includes a free set of AI job-search tools. Resumes, interview self-introductions, resignation letters, recommendation letters, and mock interview questions can all be generated on your phone from a few keywords, in both Chinese and English, with no app to download or install.

Demo: AI Career Toolbox | Build a resume on your phone

What are the AI Job Search Tools, and where can I use them?

It's a free mini app feature inside Step1ne's LINE Official Account. After adding us as a friend, open "Step1ne Referrals" from the menu or a rich message, then tap the "AI Job Search Tools" category to see all the tools. No separate app to download or install — everything works in your phone's browser.

Can I make a resume with AI on my phone? How?

Yes. Open "Smart Resume Builder" and describe your background in a few short keywords, for example: "Graduated in Electrical Engineering from National Tsing Hua University, 8 years as a product manager, previously a product manager at major tech companies, skilled in branding, user experience, and data analysis." The AI generates a complete resume with a personal summary, education, and work history. You can then fine-tune the details, and when you are happy with it, tap "Add to my resume".

How should I prepare my interview self-introduction?

With a tool called Intro Craft AI, you can generate self-introductions in two lengths, "1 minute" and "3 minutes", and adjust the length to fit the situation (for example, the short version for a phone screen and the long one for a formal interview), without having to write a script from scratch.

Are these tools available in English? Can I use them for interviews at foreign companies?

Yes. The smart resume builder, Interview Insight AI (mock interview generator), and Intro Craft AI (interview self-introduction) can all switch to English, so you can prepare for English interviews or submit an English resume without a separate translation tool.

How do I start using the AI job search tools?

  1. Add Step1ne's official LINE account
  2. Tap the menu or rich message to open Step1ne Referrals
  3. Tap the “AI job search tools” category and pick the tool you need
  4. Describe your background in a few short keywords, then wait for the AI to generate the content
  5. Review and fine-tune the details by hand, then add it to "My Resume" or copy it to use right away

Is this the same as earning a bonus by referring jobs through Step1ne Referrals?

No, but you can use them together. The AI job-search tools are free tools for job seekers to prepare their own resumes and interviews; Step1ne Referrals lets you recommend jobs to friends and earn a reward when a match is made. Both live on the same LINE Official Account, so adding us as a friend once gives you access to both.
