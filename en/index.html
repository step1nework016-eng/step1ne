FAQ

How do headhunting consultants charge?
Executive search is usually done on a contingency (success-fee) basis, with the fee typically calculated as a percentage of the hired candidate's annual salary and settled after the candidate starts. Before any engagement, Step1ne confirms the position specifications and fee structure with the company, so the process is transparent with no hidden fees.
How long does it usually take to find someone?
It depends on how hard the role is to fill. Candidates from our existing talent database and internal referral lists require zero sourcing time, so this is usually the fastest way to set up interviews; for typical roles, we start submitting candidates in about 2–4 weeks. For urgent, high-volume hiring, we can launch all channels at once (our database, job boards, LinkedIn and local communities) to fill positions faster.
Is there a guarantee period or a replacement guarantee?
We agree on a guarantee period before the engagement. If the hired candidate leaves within that period, Step1ne provides a free replacement; specific terms follow what both parties agree in the engagement contract.
Whose employee is a temporary staff member? What responsibilities does the company carry?
Staffing employees sign their employment contract with Step1ne, and Step1ne is responsible for salary, labor and health insurance, and labor-law obligations. The host company is responsible only for directing and managing the work on site. If you perform well during the assignment, both sides can separately agree on the terms and timing for conversion to a permanent position.
How is executive search different from a job board?
Job boards are passive: you post an ad and collect a wide net of résumés. Executive search is proactive: a consultant targets passive candidates in specific industries and functions (people who are employed and not openly job hunting) and approaches and evaluates them confidentially. It is well suited to key and confidential positions.
