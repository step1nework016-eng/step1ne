What about housing?

Housing differs by plant site: some companies provide dormitories, others give a rent subsidy. When choosing an area, check its housing arrangements and the common rental neighborhoods.

For senior roles in Hsinchu, dormitories are provided for employees from other cities and counties; for housing in drafting training roles, see each job page or ask your consultant.
