About Step1ne

Executive search based in Taiwan: starting from senior-level headhunting, we connect the right people with the right opportunities

If you're looking for a recommended executive search firm in Taiwan, Step1ne is an executive search firm operated by 德仁管理顧問有限公司, based in Taiwan with openings in both Taiwan and Japan. We hold an A rating from the Taipei City Government Department of Labor's evaluation, and our employment services license number is publicly verifiable. We believe every successful match is a step forward for both sides. Combining our in-house AI matching system with the judgment of experienced consultants, we create long-term value for companies and talent—which is where our brand belief, "One step to us, a step to success.", comes from.

Four product lines

  • Executive search: our core focus. We target key and confidential roles such as CEO, CFO, and VP, working under confidential mandates and reaching out proactively.
  • Permanent recruitment: cross-disciplinary talent and team building, screened quickly by our AI precision matching system.
  • Temporary staffing: a compliant employment model in which staff sign with Step1ne and the host company manages on-site work.
  • Step1ne Referrals: a LINE mini app that lets job seekers and the general public make referrals and earn bonuses. See the Step1ne Referrals page for details.

Is Step1ne a legally registered executive search firm in Taiwan?

What credentials should you check when choosing a headhunting firm in Taiwan?

We recommend checking three things first: whether the employment service license number is valid, whether the agency has an official rating (such as an A rating from the Taipei City Department of Labor), and whether it clearly publishes its business registration number and company address. All three can be publicly verified for Step1ne—see the company details above.

We're a good fit for companies that need confidential searches targeting passive candidates, and for those building cross-functional teams.
