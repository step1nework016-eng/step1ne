FAQ

Is it okay if I have no drafting or site experience at all?

Yes. All of these jobs come with company-arranged training, starting from AutoCAD 2D drawings or basic tasks. A related major or software experience is a plus but not required.

Do I have to pay for training?

No. Training is arranged by the hiring company at no cost to job seekers. Some roles require completing 6 hours of occupational safety training before starting, and that cost can be claimed from the company; see each job description for details.

Can I apply if I live in another county?

Yes. Some roles provide dormitory housing for staff from other counties, and the BIM engineer roles offer a rent subsidy. See each job page for the actual terms.

What is the application process?

Click the apply button on the job page and complete the online interview first; a consultant will then call to explain the job and terms, and once both sides agree, we arrange an interview with the hiring company. We never charge job seekers any fees.
